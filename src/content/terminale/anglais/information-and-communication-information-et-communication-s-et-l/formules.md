## Le discours indirect : structures à mémoriser

:::formule Les trois verbes introducteurs
| Verbe | Construction | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **say** | *say (that) + phrase* | *He **said (that)** he was tired.* |
| **tell** | *tell someone (that) + phrase* | *She **told me (that)** she was tired.* |
| **ask** | *ask (someone) + question indirecte* | *He **asked** me where I lived.* |
:::

:::formule Questions indirectes
- *“Where do you live ?”* → *She asked **where I lived*** (ordre normal, pas d’inversion, pas de *do/does/did*).
- *“Did you see the news ?”* → *He asked **if / whether** I had seen the news* (question fermée → *if / whether*).
- *“What time is it ?”* → *She asked **what time it was**.*
:::

:::formule Le tableau complet du backshift
| Discours direct | Discours indirect | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| présent simple | past simple | *I am tired → he **was** tired* |
| present continuous | past continuous | *I am waiting → he **was** waiting* |
| present perfect | past perfect | *I have seen → he **had** seen* |
| past simple | past perfect | *I left → he **had** left* |
| future *will* | *would* | *I will help → he **would** help* |
| *can* | *could* | *I can come → he **could** come* |
| *must* | *had to* | *I must go → he **had to** go* |
| *may* | *might* | *I may call → he **might** call* |
:::

:::remarque Quand ne pas décaler ?
On ne décale **pas** quand la phrase rapportée reste **vraie au moment où l’on parle** : *He said Paris **is** the capital of France.* (Vérité générale.) Ni après un modal inchangé : *She said she **will** come tomorrow* est possible si le futur est toujours d’actualité — mais *would* reste la forme attendue au BAC.
:::

## Repères spatio-temporels

:::formule Direct → indirect
| Direct | Indirect |
| --- | --- |
| now | then |
| today | that day |
| tomorrow | the next day / the following day |
| yesterday | the day before / the previous day |
| last week | the week before |
| next year | the following year |
| here | there |
| this / these | that / those |
| come | go |
:::

:::formule Exemple complet
*The journalist said, “I am working here today and I will publish the article tomorrow.”*
→ *The journalist said **(that)** he **was working there that day** and that he **would publish** the article **the next day**.*
:::

## Les connecteurs du discours médiatique

:::formule Citer, confirmer, nuancer
| Fonction | Expressions |
| --- | --- |
| Citer une source | *according to, as reported by, sources say, the press claims* |
| Confirmer | *indeed, in fact, as confirmed by* |
| Nuancer | *however, although, it seems that, allegedly* |
| Contredire | *on the contrary, far from it, this is false* |
| Conclure | *in short, therefore, all things considered* |
:::

:::remarque « According to » est le connecteur roi du BAC
*According to the minister, the exams will start on Monday.* (D’après le ministre…) Il introduit toujours une **source** ; avec un média : *According to the press / As reported by…*. N’oublie pas la virgule après la source.
:::
