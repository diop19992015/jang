## Prépositions de temps

:::formule At / on / in
| Préposition | Cas | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **at** | heure précise, moments | *at 7 a.m., at noon, at night, at the moment* |
| **on** | jours, dates, jours + mot | *on Tuesday, on 5 May, on Sunday morning* |
| **in** | mois, années, saisons, moments | *in Ramadan, in 2026, in summer, in the evening* |
:::

:::formule Sans préposition
Avec **last, next, this, every, tomorrow, yesterday** : aucune préposition.
→ *I saw him **last Friday*** (✗ *in last Friday*) — *See you **next week***.
:::

## Prépositions de lieu

| Idée | Préposition | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| point / adresse | **at** | *at the gate, at school* |
| intérieur | **in** | *in a box, in Senegal* |
| surface | **on** | *on the desk* |
| sous | **under / below** | *under the tree* |
| au-dessus | **above / over** | *above the clouds* |
| entre deux | **between** | *between the two schools* |
| parmi | **among** | *among the pupils* |
| à côté | **next to / beside** | *next to the bank* |
| devant / derrière | **in front of / behind** | *behind the classroom* |

## Prépositions de relation

:::formule For, since, during, until, by
- *I have studied English **for** ten years.* (durée)
- *I have studied English **since** 2016.* (départ)
- *He slept **during** the whole lecture.* (pendant)
- *Wait **until** the teacher comes.* (jusqu’à ce que)
- *The results will be out **by** Friday.* (au plus tard)
:::

## Les phrasal verbs

:::formule Les plus demandés au BAC
| Verbe | Sens | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *get up* | se lever | *I get up at six.* |
| *get on / off* | monter / descendre | *Get on the bus!* |
| *look for* | chercher | *I’m looking for my pen.* |
| *look after* | s’occuper de | *She looks after her brother.* |
| *look forward to* | attendre avec joie | *I look forward to seeing you.* |
| *give up* | abandonner | *Don’t give up.* |
| *put off* | reporter | *Put off the meeting.* |
| *turn on / off* | allumer / éteindre | *Turn off the light.* |
| *take off* | décoller / enlever | *The plane took off.* |
| *run out of* | manquer de | *We ran out of chalk.* |
| *carry on* | continuer | *Carry on, please.* |
| *break down* | tomber en panne | *The car broke down.* |
| *set up* | fonder, installer | *They set up a new clinic.* |
| *bring up* | élever | *He was brought up in Thiès.* |
:::

:::formule Séparable ou inséparable ?
- **Séparable** : *turn off the light* / *turn **the light** off* / ***turn it off*** (le pronom est **toujours** entre le verbe et la particule).
- **Inséparable** : *look after the children* — le pronom ne peut pas s’intercaler : ✗ *look them after* → ✓ *look **after** them*.
:::

:::remarque Choisir la bonne préposition
Il n’existe pas de logique universelle : les prépositions s’apprennent **avec l’expression**. Apprends *depend **on**, be afraid **of**, good **at**, interested **in**, proud **of*** comme des blocs entiers.
:::
