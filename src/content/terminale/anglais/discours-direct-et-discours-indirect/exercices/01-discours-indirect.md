---
titre: Transformer le discours direct en discours indirect
difficulte: 1
type: entrainement
indices:
  - Repère le verbe de report et le type de phrase : déclaration, question, ordre.
  - Applique le recul des temps, puis change les pronoms et les marques de temps.
  - Pour les questions : ordre sujet + verbe, sans inversion, avec if/whether ou le mot interrogatif.
---

## Énoncé

**A.** Mettez les phrases suivantes au discours indirect.

1. *Aminata said, “I am busy today.”*
2. *The teacher said, “You have finished the exercise.”*
3. *Moussa said, “I will call you tomorrow.”*
4. *She said, “I saw that film last week.”*

**B.** Transformez les questions au discours indirect.

5. *He asked, “Do you live in Saint-Louis?”*
6. *She asked, “Where are you going now?”*
7. *The teacher asked, “Why haven’t you done your homework?”*

**C.** Transformez les ordres et les demandes.

8. *“Don’t be late for the exam,” said the headmaster.*
9. *“Please help me with this exercise,” she said.*
10. *“You should see a doctor,” the nurse said.*

**D.** Corrigez les phrases fausses.

11. He told that he was tired.
12. She asked me where was I going.

## Solution

**A.**

1. ***Aminata said she was busy that day.***
   Présent continu → past continuous (*was busy*), *I → she*, *today → that day*.
2. ***The teacher said (that) we had finished the exercise.***
   Present perfect → past perfect (*had finished*), *you → we* (contexte de classe) ou *you* conservé si le tutoiement s’adresse aux élèves : *you had finished*.
3. ***Moussa said he would call me the next day.***
   *will → would*, *you → me*, *tomorrow → the next day*.
4. ***She said she had seen that film the week before.***
   Past simple → past perfect (*had seen*), *last week → the week before*.

**B.**

5. ***He asked me if I lived in Saint-Louis.***
   Question Oui/Non → *asked + if* ; ordre sujet + verbe (*if I lived*) ; présent simple → past simple.
6. ***She asked me where I was going then.***
   Mot interrogatif conservé (*where*) ; *are going → was going* ; *now → then* ; aucune inversion.
7. ***The teacher asked why I hadn’t done my homework.***
   *Why* introduit la question ; present perfect → past perfect négatif (*hadn’t done*) ; ordre *I hadn’t done*.

**C.**

8. ***The headmaster told us not to be late for the exam.***
   Ordre négatif → *told + personne + not to + infinitif*.
9. ***She asked me to help her with this exercise.***
   Demande polie → *asked me to help* ; *me → her* pour le pronom de la personne aidée.
10. ***The nurse advised me to see a doctor.***
   Conseil → *advised + personne + to + infinitif*.

**D.**

11. ***He said that he was tired.*** ou ***He told me that he was tired.***
    *Tell* exige toujours une personne après lui ; sans personne, on utilise *say*.
12. ***She asked me where I was going.***
    Au discours indirect, on revient à l’ordre **sujet + verbe** : *where I was going*, sans inversion.

:::remarque Vérification
Relis la phrase rapportée : les temps ont-ils reculé ? Les pronoms et les marqueurs de temps ont-ils changé ? L’ordre est-il celui d’une affirmative ?
:::
