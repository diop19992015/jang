## Les structures à mémoriser

:::formule Les quatre structures
| Type | Structure | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Déclaration | verbe de report + **(that)** + sujet + verbe | *He said **(that) he was** tired.* |
| Question Oui/Non | asked + **if / whether** + sujet + verbe | *She asked **if I could** help.* |
| Question interrogative | asked + **mot interrogatif** + sujet + verbe | *He asked **where I lived**.* |
| Ordre / demande | told / asked + personne + **(not) to** + infinitif | *She told me **not to worry**.* |
:::

:::remarque L’ordre des mots est la clé
Au discours indirect, la phrase redevient une **affirmative** : sujet d’abord, verbe ensuite, **sans inversion** et sans auxiliaire *do*.
:::

## Le tableau des temps (backshift)

| Discours direct | Discours indirect |
| --- | --- |
| *I **work** every day.* | *He said he **worked** every day.* |
| *I **am working** now.* | *She said she **was working** then.* |
| *I **have finished**.* | *He said he **had finished**.* |
| *I **finished** yesterday.* | *She said she **had finished** the day before.* |
| *I **will call** you.* | *He said he **would call** me.* |
| *I **can** swim.* | *She said she **could** swim.* |
| *You **must** go.* | *He said I **had to** go.* |
| *The earth **is** round.* | *He said the earth **is** round* (vérité générale : inchangé). |

## Les changements de mots

| Discours direct | Discours indirect |
| --- | --- |
| I / we | he / she / they |
| my / our | his / her / their |
| me / us | him / them |
| now | then, at that time |
| today | that day |
| yesterday | the day before, the previous day |
| tomorrow | the next day, the following day |
| tonight | that night |
| here | there |
| this / these | that / those |
| last week | the week before |

## Les verbes de report

| Sens | Verbes | Construction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dire | *say, state, answer* | *said (that)…* |
| Raconter à quelqu’un | *tell* | *told + personne + (that)…* |
| Demander | *ask, wonder* | *asked (if / wh)…* |
| Ordonner | *order, instruct* | *ordered sb **to**…* |
| Conseiller | *advise, recommend* | *advised sb **to**…* |
| Prévenir | *warn, remind* | *warned sb **to**…* |
| Promettre | *promise* | *promised **to**…* |
| Suggérer | *suggest, propose* | *suggested **doing / that**…* (jamais *to*) |

:::formule Rappel des deux pièges
$$
\text{say (sans personne)} \quad / \quad \text{tell + personne}
$$

- *He **said** he was busy.* — *He **told me** he was busy.*
- Négation : *He **didn’t say** anything.* — *He **didn’t tell me** anything.*
:::
