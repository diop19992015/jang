## Question

Comment rapporte-t-on la phrase « She said, “I am revising my lesson now.” » ?

- [ ] She said I am revising my lesson now.
- [x] She said she was revising her lesson then.
- [ ] She said she is revising her lesson now.
- [ ] She told she was revising her lesson then.

> Le recul des temps transforme le présent continu en **past continuous** (*was revising*), le pronom *I* en *she*, *my* en *her* et *now* en *then*. La proposition 4 utilise mal *tell*, qui exige une personne (*told me*).

## Question

Quelle est la forme correcte de la question « “Did you see my bag?” » au discours indirect ?

- [ ] He asked me did I see her bag.
- [x] He asked me if I had seen her bag.
- [ ] He asked me if had I seen her bag.
- [ ] He said me if I saw her bag.

> Question Oui/Non → **asked + if**, ordre **sujet + verbe** (*if I had seen*) avec recul : *did you see* (past simple) → *had seen* (past perfect). Ni inversion, ni *did*, ni *said me*.

## Question

Que devient « tomorrow » dans « He said, “I will come tomorrow.” » ?

- [ ] yesterday
- [x] the next day
- [ ] that day
- [ ] now

> *Tomorrow* (demain) devient **the next day** (le lendemain) au discours indirect. *That day* correspondrait à *today*, et le temps du verbe passerait de *will* à *would*.

## Question

Quelle phrase est correctement formée avec un ordre au discours indirect ?

- [ ] The teacher told us to not talk.
- [ ] The teacher said us to open our books.
- [x] The teacher told us to open our books.
- [ ] The teacher told us not open our books.

> La structure est **told + personne + to + infinitif** : *told us to open*. La négation se place en *not to* : *told us **not to** talk*. *Say* ne prend jamais de personne complément.
