## L’essentiel du chapitre

:::formule À quoi servent les linking words ?
Relier deux idées et montrer leur relation : **ajouter, situer dans le temps, expliquer, conclure, opposer, conditionner**.
:::

:::formule Addition et temps
- Addition : *and, also, moreover, in addition, what is more*
- Temps : *first, then, next, after that, finally, while, when*
:::

:::formule Cause, conséquence, but
| Relation | Mots | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cause + phrase | *because, since, as* | *We stayed home **because** it rained.* |
| Cause + nom | *because of, due to* | ***Because of** the rain, we stayed home.* |
| Conséquence | *so, therefore, as a result* | *He revised well, **so** he passed.* |
| But | *so that, in order to* | *I woke up early **in order to** catch the bus.* |
:::

:::formule Opposition et concession
| Relation | Mots | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opposition | *but, while, whereas* | *He is small **but** strong.* |
| Concession + phrase | *although, even though, though* | ***Although** he was tired, he worked.* |
| Concession + nom | *in spite of, despite* | ***In spite of** the heat, we played.* |
| Début de phrase | *however, nevertheless, still* | *It was cold. **However,** we played.* |
:::

:::formule Condition
*if* (si), *unless* (sauf si), *as long as* (à condition que), *otherwise* (sinon), *in case* (au cas où).
*If you don’t revise, you will fail.* = *Unless you revise, you will fail.*
:::

## Méthode express

1. Lis les deux idées et pose la question : quelle relation ? (temps, cause, conséquence, but, opposition, condition…)
2. Choisis le mot de liaison dans les tableaux.
3. Vérifie la construction : *because / although* + **phrase** ; *because of / in spite of* + **nom ou -ing**.
4. Vérifie la ponctuation : *however, therefore, moreover* ouvrent une phrase, suivis d’une **virgule**.
5. Jamais deux liens pour une même relation : ni *although… but*, ni *because… so*.

:::erreur Les erreurs qui coûtent des points
1. ~~*Although he was tired, but he worked*~~ → *Although he was tired, he worked.*
2. ~~*Because of it rained*~~ → *Because it rained.* / *Because of the rain.*
3. ~~*In spite of he was poor*~~ → *In spite of his poverty* / *In spite of being poor.*
4. ~~*However he was tired*~~ → *However, he worked.* (phrase distincte + virgule)
5. ~~*Unless you will not revise*~~ → *Unless you revise, you will fail.*
:::

## Vocabulaire

- **Linking word** : mot de liaison.
- **Addition** : adding another idea (*and, moreover*).
- **Cause** : why something happens (*because, since*).
- **Consequence** : what happens as a result (*so, therefore*).
- **Purpose** : the aim (*so that, in order to*).
- **Opposition** : a contrasting idea (*but, while*).
- **Concession** : admitting a difficulty (*although, in spite of*).
- **Condition** : a requirement (*if, unless*).
