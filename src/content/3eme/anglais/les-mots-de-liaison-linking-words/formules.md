## Les mots de liaison par fonction

### Addition et énumération

| Fonction | Mots de liaison | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Addition simple | *and, also, as well as* | *He plays football **and** basketball.* |
| Addition (soutenu) | *moreover, furthermore, in addition, what is more* | ***Moreover,** the room was clean.* |
| Énumération | *first, second, then, next, after that, finally* | ***First** work, **then** play.* |

### Temps et simultanéité

| Fonction | Mots de liaison | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Avant | *before, until, up to* | *Finish **before** the bell rings.* |
| Après | *after, afterwards, then, later* | ***After** lunch, we revised.* |
| En même temps | *while, when, as, meanwhile* | ***While** I cooked, she read.* |
| Dès que | *as soon as, once* | ***As soon as** he arrived, we left.* |
| Fin | *at last, in the end, finally* | ***Finally** the results were announced.* |

### Cause, conséquence, but

| Fonction | Mots de liaison | Construction | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cause | *because, since, as* | + phrase | *We stayed home **because** it rained.* |
| Cause | *because of, due to, owing to* | + nom | ***Because of** the rain, we stayed home.* |
| Conséquence | *so, therefore, as a result, consequently* | + phrase | *He worked hard, **so** he succeeded.* |
| But | *so that* | + phrase | *She spoke slowly **so that** we understood.* |
| But | *in order to, so as to* | + verbe nu | *I got up early **in order to** catch the bus.* |

### Opposition et concession

| Fonction | Mots de liaison | Construction | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Opposition | *but, yet, while, whereas* | au milieu de la phrase | *He is poor **but** happy.* |
| Concession | *although, even though, though* | + phrase | ***Although** he was tired, he worked.* |
| Concession | *in spite of, despite* | + nom ou *-ing* | ***In spite of** the rain, we played.* |
| Concession | *however, nevertheless, still, yet* | début de phrase + virgule | *It was raining. **However,** we went out.* |

### Condition et conséquence négative

| Fonction | Mots de liaison | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Condition | *if* | *If you revise, you will pass.* |
| Sauf si | *unless* (= if… not) | *You will fail **unless** you work.* |
| À condition que | *as long as, provided that* | *You may go **as long as** you finish.* |
| Sinon | *otherwise* | *Hurry up, **otherwise** you will miss the bus.* |
| Au cas où | *in case* | *Take an umbrella **in case** it rains.* |

## Règles de construction

:::formule Les paires à ne pas mélanger
$$
\text{because / although / while} + \text{phrase (sujet + verbe)}
$$
$$
\text{because of / in spite of / despite} + \text{nom ou verbe en -ing}
$$
Exemples : *because he was late* / *because of his lateness* ; *although it rained* / *in spite of the rain* / *in spite of raining*.
:::

:::remarque *However* n’est pas *but*
*however* est un adverbe : il ne relie pas deux propositions dans la même phrase. Il ouvre une nouvelle phrase, toujours suivi d’une virgule.
- Correct : *He was tired. **However,** he continued.*
- Faux : *He was tired however he continued.*
:::

:::remarque *So* et *because* ne vont pas ensemble
On dit *It rained, **so** we stayed* **ou** ***Because** it rained, we stayed*, jamais les deux en même temps pour la même relation.
:::
