## Énoncé

Le texte suivant est tiré d’un sujet de BFEM. Complétez les blancs avec les mots de liaison suivants : *because, however, so, although, in spite of, in order to*.

« Every Saturday, Khady goes to the market with her mother. (1) ______ she is only twelve, she carries the heavy bags. The market is far from their house, (2) ______ they leave very early. (3) ______ the noise and the heat, Khady enjoys these visits. She learns the prices of fish and vegetables. (4) ______ she cannot bargain yet, her mother teaches her. Khady saves a little money (5) ______ buy books for school. (6) ______, she has become a good helper. »

**Partie A.** (3 points)
1. Complétez les six blancs avec les mots de liaison proposés.

**Partie B.** (4 points)
2. Classez chaque mot de liaison utilisé : cause, conséquence, concession, but.
3. Donnez un synonyme possible pour chaque blanc.

**Partie C.** (3 points)
4. Rédigez un paragraphe de 4 à 5 phrases sur « My Saturday ». Vous utiliserez au moins trois mots de liaison différents : un de cause, un d’opposition et un de but.

## Solution

**1. Complétion (3 points — 0,5 point chacun)**

(1) ***Although*** — *Although she is only twelve, she carries the heavy bags.*
(2) ***so*** — *…the market is far, **so** they leave very early.*
(3) ***In spite of*** — *In spite of the noise and the heat, Khady enjoys these visits.*
(4) ***Although*** — *Although she cannot bargain yet, her mother teaches her.* (ou *but* : *…yet, **but** her mother teaches her* — le sens reste correct)
(5) ***in order to*** — *She saves a little money **in order to** buy books for school.*
(6) ***However*** — *However, she has become a good helper.*

**2. Classification (4 points — 0,5 point chacun)**

| Blanc | Mot de liaison | Fonction | Synonyme possible |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| (1) | *although* | **concession** | *even though, though* |
| (2) | *so* | **conséquence** | *therefore, as a result* |
| (3) | *in spite of* | **concession** (+ nom) | *despite, notwithstanding* |
| (4) | *although* | **concession** | *while, even though* |
| (5) | *in order to* | **but** | *so as to, to* |
| (6) | *however* | **opposition / transition** | *nevertheless, yet* |

Justifications : *although* + phrase complète (concession) ; *in spite of* + nom (*the noise*) ; *so* marque la conséquence de l’éloignement du marché ; *in order to* + verbe nu introduit le but ; *however* ouvre une phrase et oppose la difficulté initiale à la réussite finale.

**3. Rédaction (3 points)**

Critères de correction : 1 point pour l’utilisation correcte de **trois** mots de liaison différents, 1 point pour la justesse des relations (cause, opposition, but), 1 point pour la correction grammaticale et la cohérence du texte.

Modèle :
*Every Saturday, I help my father in the garage. **Because** the garage is near our house, we walk there. **Although** the work is hard, I enjoy it. I learn to repair bikes **in order to** save money for my school fees. **However**, I always find time to play football with my friends in the evening.*

:::erreur Piège classique
*In spite of* n’est **jamais** suivi d’une phrase complète : on écrit *in spite of **the heat*** ou *in spite of **being hot***, jamais ~~in spite of it was hot~~. C’est l’erreur la plus fréquente de cette leçon.
:::
