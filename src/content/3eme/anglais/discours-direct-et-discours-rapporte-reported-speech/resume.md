## L’essentiel du chapitre

:::formule Les trois grandes transformations
| Type | Verbe introducteur | Construction | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Affirmation | *say / tell* | said / told (+ personne) **(that)** + phrase | *He said (that) he was tired.* |
| Question fermée | *ask* | asked (+ personne) **if / whether** + phrase affirmative | *He asked if I was ready.* |
| Question ouverte | *ask* | asked (+ personne) **mot interrogatif** + phrase affirmative | *She asked where I lived.* |
| Ordre / demande | *tell / ask / order* | told / asked (+ personne) **to + V** | *He told me to close the door.* |
:::

:::formule Le décalage des temps (backshift)
| Direct | Rapporté | Direct | Rapporté |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| *am / is* | *was* | *will* | *would* |
| *are* | *were* | *can* | *could* |
| *present simple* | *past simple* | *must* | *had to* |
| *present continuous* | *past continuous* | *do / does* | *did* |
| *past simple* | *past perfect* | *has / have* | *had* |
| *present perfect* | *past perfect* | *may* | *might* |
:::

:::formule Les mots qui changent
*now* → *then* · *today* → *that day* · *tomorrow* → *the next day* · *yesterday* → *the day before* · *here* → *there* · *this* → *that* · *last week* → *the week before*
:::

:::formule Say ou tell ?
- *He **said** (that) he was busy.* (Il a dit qu’il était occupé.) — **say** + chose.
- *He **told me** (that) he was busy.* (Il m’a dit qu’il était occupé.) — **tell** + personne.
:::

## Méthode express

1. Repérer le **type** : affirmation, question (fermée ou ouverte) ou impératif.
2. Choisir le **verbe introducteur** et le mettre au passé (*said, told, asked, ordered*).
3. Choisir le **connecteur** : *that* / *if ou whether* / le mot interrogatif / *to + infinitif*.
4. **Remplacer les pronoms** : *I* → *he ou she*, *you* → *I ou we*, *my* → *his ou her*.
5. **Reculer les temps** d’un cran et changer les mots de temps / lieu.
6. Remettre l’**ordre direct** (plus d’inversion) et supprimer les guillemets.

:::erreur Les erreurs qui coûtent des points
1. ~~*He said he is tired*~~ → *He said he **was** tired* (décalage obligatoire).
2. ~~*She asked where did I live*~~ → *She asked **where I lived*** (ordre direct).
3. ~~*He asked I was ready*~~ → *He asked **if I was** ready*.
4. ~~*He said me…*~~ / ~~*He told that…*~~ → *He **told me*** / *He **said***.
5. ~~*told me not go*~~ → *told me **not to go***.
6. ~~*He said he would come tomorrow*~~ → *…**the next day***.
:::

## Vocabulaire

- **direct speech** : le discours direct (paroles exactes, guillemets).
- **reported / indirect speech** : le discours rapporté (le même sens sans guillemets).
- **a reporting verb** : un verbe introducteur (*say, tell, ask, order*).
- **to report** : rapporter, raconter.
- **a backshift** : le décalage des temps.
- **a pronoun** : un pronom (*I, you, he, my*).
- **a question word** : un mot interrogatif (*what, where, why, how*).
- **to order** : ordonner ; **to ask** : demander ; **to warn** : avertir ; **to advise** : conseiller.
- **whether / if** : si (dans les questions rapportées).
