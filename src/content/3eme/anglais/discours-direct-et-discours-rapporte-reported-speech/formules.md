## Discours direct → discours rapporté : les structures

:::formule Une affirmation
**Sujet + said / told (+ personne) + (that) + phrase à l’ordre direct**
| Discours direct | Discours rapporté |
| --- | --- |
| *“I am tired,” he said.* | *He said **(that)** he **was** tired.* |
| *“I have finished,” she said.* | *She said **(that)** she **had** finished.* |
| *“I will come tomorrow,” they said.* | *They said **(that)** they **would** come **the next day**.* |
| *“We live in Dakar,” he said.* | *He told me **(that)** he **lived** in Dakar.* |
:::

:::formule Une question fermée (oui / non)
**Sujet + asked (+ personne) + if / whether + phrase à l’ordre direct**
| Discours direct | Discours rapporté |
| --- | --- |
| *“Are you ready?” he asked.* | *He asked **if** I **was** ready.* |
| *“Did you pass?” the teacher asked.* | *The teacher asked **whether** we **had passed**.* |
| *“Have you seen my bag?” she asked.* | *She asked **if** I **had seen** her bag.* |
:::

:::formule Une question ouverte (mot interrogatif)
**Sujet + asked (+ personne) + mot interrogatif + phrase à l’ordre direct**
| Discours direct | Discours rapporté |
| --- | --- |
| *“Where do you live?” she asked.* | *She asked **where I lived**.* |
| *“What are you doing?” he asked.* | *He asked **what I was doing**.* |
| *“Why are you late?” the teacher asked.* | *The teacher asked **why I was late**.* |
| *“How old are you?” he asked.* | *He asked **how old I was**.* |
:::

:::formule Un ordre ou une demande (impératif)
**Sujet + told / ordered / asked (+ personne) + to + infinitif (not to pour la négation)**
| Discours direct | Discours rapporté |
| --- | --- |
| *“Close the door,” he said.* | *He **told** us **to close** the door.* |
| *“Stand up!” the teacher said.* | *The teacher **ordered** the pupils **to stand up**.* |
| *“Please help me,” Fatou said.* | *Fatou **asked** me **to help** her.* |
| *“Don’t be late,” she said.* | *She **asked** us **not to be** late.* |
:::

## Le décalage des temps (backshift)

:::formule Le tableau complet
| Temps du discours direct | Temps rapporté | Exemple direct | Exemple rapporté |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| present simple | past simple | *I **am** late.* | *He said he **was** late.* |
| present continuous | past continuous | *I **am waiting**.* | *She said she **was waiting**.* |
| past simple | past perfect | *I **missed** the bus.* | *He said he **had missed** the bus.* |
| present perfect | past perfect | *I **have finished**.* | *She said she **had finished**.* |
| past perfect | past perfect (inchangé) | *I **had left**.* | *He said he **had left**.* |
| will | would | *I **will pass**.* | *She said she **would pass**.* |
| can | could | *I **can** swim.* | *He said he **could** swim.* |
| must | had to | *You **must** revise.* | *He said I **had to** revise.* |
| may | might | *It **may** rain.* | *She said it **might** rain.* |
| do / does | did | *I **do** my work.* | *He said he **did** his work.* |
| has / have | had | *She **has** left.* | *He said she **had** left.* |
| am / is / are | was / were | *We **are** here.* | *They said they **were** there.* |
:::

:::formule Les mots qui changent
| Discours direct | Discours rapporté |
| --- | --- |
| *now* | *then* |
| *today* | *that day* |
| *tomorrow / the day after* | *the next day / the following day* |
| *yesterday* | *the day before* |
| *last night / last week* | *the night before / the week before* |
| *next year* | *the following year* |
| *here* | *there* |
| *this* | *that* |
| *these* | *those* |
:::

:::remarque Quand on ne change rien
Les vérités générales et les faits toujours vrais gardent leur temps : *“The earth goes round the sun,” he said.* → *He said that the earth **goes** round the sun.* De même, si la parole vient de se faire, le décalage est facultatif : *He said he **is** here (now)* reste acceptable.
:::

## Les verbes introducteurs

| Verbe | Construction | Exemple rapporté |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *say* | said (that) + phrase | *He said (that) he was ill.* |
| *tell* | told + personne + (that) + phrase | *He told **me** (that) he was ill.* |
| *ask* | asked (+ personne) + if / wh- / to + V | *She asked me to wait.* |
| *order* | ordered + personne + to + V | *The teacher ordered us to sit down.* |
| *advise* | advised + personne + to + V | *The doctor advised him to rest.* |
| *warn* | warned + personne (not) to + V | *She warned us not to go out.* |
| *invite* | invited + personne + to + V | *They invited me to the ceremony.* |
| *beg* | begged + personne + to + V | *He begged his mother to stay.* |
| *promise* | promised (+ personne) + to + V | *She promised to help us.* |
| *refuse* | refused + to + V | *He refused to answer.* |

:::formule Règle d’or say / tell
**say** + chose (*said something*) ; **tell** + personne (*told somebody*) .
~~*He said me*~~ et ~~*He told that*~~ sont toujours faux.
:::

## Méthode de transformation en 5 étapes

1. Verbe introducteur → au **passé** (*says → said, asks → asked*).
2. Connecteur : **that** (affirmation) / **if ou whether** (question fermée) / **mot interrogatif** (question ouverte) / **to + infinitif** (impératif).
3. Pronoms : *I → he ou she*, *you → I ou we*, *my → his ou her*, *our → their*.
4. Temps reculés d’un cran + mots de temps / lieu changés.
5. Ordre **direct** dans les questions, plus de guillemets, relecture.
