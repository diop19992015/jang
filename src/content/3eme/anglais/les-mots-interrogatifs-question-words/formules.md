## Le tableau des mots interrogatifs

:::formule Mot interrogatif → information demandée
| Mot | Information | Exemple | Exemple de réponse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| *what* | chose, fait, métier | *What do you do?* | *I am a pupil.* |
| *who* | personne (sujet) | *Who came first?* | *Moussa did.* |
| *whom* | personne (après une préposition) | *Who(m) are you talking to?* | *To my cousin.* |
| *whose* | possession | *Whose phone is this?* | *It is Khady’s.* |
| *which* | choix limité | *Which dress do you like?* | *The red one.* |
| *where* | lieu | *Where is the station?* | *Over there.* |
| *when* | temps | *When does it start?* | *At nine.* |
| *why* | cause | *Why are you crying?* | *Because I lost my keys.* |
| *how* | manière | *How did you come?* | *By taxi.* |
:::

:::formule Les combinaisons avec « how »
| Expression | Information | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *how many* + nom comptable pluriel | nombre | *How many chairs are there?* |
| *how much* + nom non comptable | quantité | *How much water do you drink?* |
| *how much* + prix | prix | *How much does it cost?* |
| *how old* | âge | *How old is your brother?* |
| *how long* | durée / longueur | *How long is the film?* |
| *how often* | fréquence | *How often do you revise?* |
| *how far* | distance | *How far is Saint-Louis?* |
| *how many times* | nombre de fois | *How many times did you fail?* |
:::

:::remarque *What* ou *which* ?
- *What* : réponse ouverte, sans choix imposé → *What do you want?*
- *which* : réponse parmi **deux ou plusieurs possibilités** → *Which do you want, the blue one or the red one?*
:::

## La formation des questions

:::formule Les 4 types de phrases
- Phrase affirmative : **sujet + verbe** → *He is tall.*
- Question oui/non : **auxiliaire + sujet + verbe** → ***Is** he tall?*
- Question à un mot : **mot interrogatif + auxiliaire + sujet + verbe** → ***How tall is** he?*
- Question indirecte : **mot interrogatif + sujet + verbe** → *I know **how tall he is**.*

| Type de verbe | Exemple affirmatif | Question oui/non | Question à un mot |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| *be* | *He **is** tall.* | ***Is** he tall?* | ***How tall is** he?* |
| *have* (possessif) | *She **has** a bike.* | ***Does** she have a bike?* | ***What does** she have?* |
| modal | *They **can** swim.* | ***Can** they swim?* | ***Who can** swim?* |
| verbe ordinaire | *We **play** football.* | ***Do** you play football?* | ***When do** you play?* |
| passé simple | *He **wrote** a letter.* | ***Did** he write a letter?* | ***Who did** he write to?* |
:::

:::formule Le rôle de do / does / did
- *do* : sujet *I, you, we, they* → *Do you like rice?*
- *does* : sujet *he, she, it* → *Does she live here?*
- *did* : passé simple, tous les sujets → *Did they come yesterday?*
- Après *do/does/did* : **base verbale** + le reste de la phrase reste au **passé** si *did*.
:::

## Correspondance direct → indirect

:::formule Pas d’inversion dans la question indirecte
| Question directe | Verbe d’introduction | Question indirecte |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Where **is** the school?* | *know* | *I know **where the school is**.* |
| *When **did** you leave?* | *ask* | *She asked **when you left**.* |
| *Why **are** they late?* | *tell me* | *Tell me **why they are late**.* |
| *How much **does** it cost?* | *wonder* | *I wonder **how much it cost**.* |
| *Is he coming?* | *don’t know* | *I don’t know **if he is coming**.* |
:::

:::remarque Les mots qui appellent une question
- *because* → **why** · *by bus / on foot* → **how** · *in Dakar* → **where** · *yesterday / last year* → **when** · *a book* → **what** · *Aminata’s* → **whose** · *thirty* → **how many** · *yes / no* → question **oui/non** (aucun mot interrogatif).
:::

## Le plan d’une question notée au BFEM

1. Quel **mot interrogatif** ? (ou aucun, si la réponse est oui/non)
2. Quel **auxiliaire** dans la phrase ? (*be*, modal, *do/does/did*)
3. **Inversion** auxiliaire + sujet.
4. **Base verbale** du verbe principal (et temps conservé si *did*).
5. **?** et intonation montante.

:::formule Vérification rapide
Question indirecte → je **retire** l’inversion et je remets l’ordre normal : *Do you know **where he lives**?*
:::
